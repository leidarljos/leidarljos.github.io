#+title: Reloading a pack is not a check.
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

[[file:index.org][Back to the field]]

=ljos policy -- argv= prints the line. It does not reload a Janet pack.

Edit the pack only when argv policy itself changes. Do not write comments, dates, ticket ids, or sitting notes into it. If sessiond is down, leave the pack alone.

Other projects may speak one habitat and ignore =ljos=.
