Answer accuracy on the same 470 uses a local 7 B reader and judge (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q5_K_M, LongMemEval prompts, top five sessions). CombMNZ fusion is 0.549. Lexical alone is 0.532. Labelled sessions, the ceiling for any retriever, are 0.634.

MemoryAgentBench (doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.05257), reader Qwen2.5-7B. Accurate retrieval scores each source on its own metric, then takes the mean of the four. Lexical job 11759 is 0.568 (95% CI 0.530-0.605, clustered by question). The per-source scores are SH 0.730, MH 0.530, LongMemEval (S*) 0.370 and EventQA 0.640. Published rows are BM25 0.605 and HippoRAG-v2 0.651 with a GPT-4o-mini reader. Conflict resolution at 262K is 0.395 for fused live (job 11750, CI 0.350-0.440) and 0.240 for lexical. The published FactConsolidation rows are BM25 0.255 and HippoRAG-v2 0.295. Hop2 (job 11793) is about 0.555 from the job log, with its verdicts held out. The scorer and verdict files are private; I share them on request.

Neighbouring published numbers

Two public repositories share the name agentmemory. They report different protocols. The first is session retrieval (R@5). The second is question answering with a hosted generator and a hosted judge. packset publishes both a retrieval row and a local-reader QA row so those protocols stay distinct.

The 0.949 fused recall@5 is the neighbour of the 95.2% R@5 retrieval figure. The 96.20% QA figure uses a different generator and a different judge. InjecMEM and the Memory INJection Attack (MINJA) describe the risk of writing from a transcript. packset writes only on Remember: and Prefer:. Privacy-of-write.

policyd is argv law

ljos policy -- argv prints the line and any pack rule that matches it. ljos rule writes that law into the pack. ljos-policyd is the trusted computing base when it is on PATH or POLICYD_BIN: it prints allow or deny. Reloading a pack is not a check. ljos-policyd docs · Argv field note.
