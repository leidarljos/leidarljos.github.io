The loop

Write. Sit. Finish.

01

Remember

One claim, two sentences at most, stored as written. ljos remember and ljos prefer are the only pack writes. Nothing is mined from a transcript.

02

Sit

ljos sitting opens: doctor, cards, due, island, recall, claim. Cite what the work produced. Completing here does not close the ticket.

03

Finish

ljos finish remembers the lesson, fires the island, completes the session node, and learns from an outcome when one is named.
